# Example: Realistic Adult Portrait Contract

Use this as a prompt starting point. Replace the placeholders and do not use it to clone a real person's identity.

```text
Use case: realistic-photography
Playbook: realistic-portrait
Asset type: personal exploration portrait

Reference roles:
- Image A controls only vertical head-and-shoulders composition, camera distance, eye placement, gaze, visual age, and face proportions.
- Image B controls only natural light, real location context, camera color, lens depth of field, skin/hair texture, and photographic grain.
- Ignore exact identity, clothing, location, watermarks, text, logos, and source-specific objects from both references.

Create an original portrait of a visually around-20-year-old, clearly adult Chinese woman with youthful freshness but no childlike coding. She looks naturally at the camera with a relaxed, slight smile. She wears everyday casual clothing, not a school uniform.

The scene is a real spring morning on a tree-lined city street. Soft side-front daylight moves through leaves and storefronts. The image should feel like a friend's candid 50mm f/2.0 full-frame photograph, not a studio portrait.

Include natural pores, subtle skin tone variation, flyaway hairs, real eyebrow and eyelash fibers, practical background detail, natural shallow depth of field, slight film grain, and mild handheld imperfection.

Text: none.
Avoid: plastic skin, over-smoothed skin, beauty filter, AI portrait, studio light, fake rim light, dreamy bokeh, perfect symmetry, heavy makeup, minor, child, school uniform, sexualized pose, nudity, watermark, logo, text, cartoon, anime, 3D render, malformed eyes, teeth, or ears.
```
